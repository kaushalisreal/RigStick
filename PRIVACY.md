# RigStick privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

RigStick is a desktop animation app. Your animations, projects, images, audio and transcripts stay on your
device. RigStick has no account system, no advertising and no third-party analytics.

## What RigStick sends over the internet

RigStick only connects to the internet for these features:

| Feature | When | What is sent | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Update check (installer version only) | Shortly after the app starts, then once an hour, and when you choose Check for updates | The app version, in a normal download request | GitHub (github.com) |
| Transcription engine and models | Only when you first use **Transcribe** and choose a model | A normal download request | GitHub (whisper.cpp releases) and Hugging Face (huggingface.co) |
| Optional usage statistics and diagnostics | Only if you allow them in **Privacy**; off until you choose | Anonymous usage counts and performance timings: no animation contents, file names, paths, text, clipboard, key presses, screenshots or recordings | The RigStick diagnostics service |

The Microsoft Store version gets its updates from the Store and does not run its own update check.

You can change this at any time in the app's **Privacy** settings. Turning them off stops sending.

## AI connection (MCP)

RigStick can let AI apps that you install (for example Claude Desktop or Gemini CLI) control the open
animation. This connection listens only on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`) and needs a private key that
RigStick stores on your device. RigStick itself sends nothing to any AI service. What your AI app does with
the data it reads is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

## Data stored on your device

RigStick keeps your settings, recent-files list, saved library items, downloaded transcription models and
crash-recovery copies in your user profile folder. Uninstalling RigStick removes the app. Your saved
projects stay wherever you saved them.

## Children

RigStick does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

# RigStick 1.3.1

Faster exports, preset files and a Hardware tab

### Added

- Save any preset as its own file: .rigset for animations and effects, .rigobject for objects, stickers, text looks and figures.
- Make your own presets from the canvas: save a selection as an object preset, or a figure's animation or effects as a preset.
- Import .rigset and .rigobject files, packs and images into My presets, kept as files in Documents\RigStick\Presets.
- Ninja VFX Pack: manga sound effects, animated slashes, impacts and smoke, a shuriken, Japanese cars and golden-hour Tokyo backgrounds.
- Settings › Hardware: choose the graphics card, the MP4 video encoder and the audio output, and see your processor and memory.
- Window menu: show or close any panel, or reset the panel layout.
- Each panel's ⋯ menu closes it, floats it or docks it on the other side.
- Right-click empty space in the timeline's layer list to add a layer, a drawing layer or a folder.
- Presets land on the layer you pick: drop one on a layer in the timeline, or select the layer first.
- The 3D Shot studio shows how to ask your AI app to build 3D shots for you.

### Changed

- The Library is one view: your saved items first, then every preset, filtered by Show and search.
- Recent releases in What's new opens Rigstick in the Microsoft Store.
- Rigstick uses the dedicated graphics card on computers that have two.
- Exports keep their full speed when the window is minimized or covered.
- Plainer notification popups, and no more Reduced preview label over the stage.

### Fixed

- Fixed exports failing at the moment the file was saved.
- Fixed a preset card's ⋯ button applying the preset instead of opening its menu.
- Fixed imported presets disappearing after a restart.

### Performance

- Animations with large background images export several times faster, with or without motion blur.

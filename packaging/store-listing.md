## Description

RigStick is a free stick figure animator for Windows.

Pose figures with real joints and IK, key them on a timeline, move the camera, and add effects, all
without starting from scratch: the preset library has more than 400 ready-made pieces you drag onto the
canvas or apply with one click.

PRESET LIBRARY
- 113 objects: weapons, props, vehicles, nature, buildings and more
- 58 character moves: walk, run, jump, punch, kick, wave, fall, sit and others
- 51 motions for any object or text: entrances, exits, bounces, shakes, loops and paths
- Camera moves: zooms, pans, shakes and follow
- 91 effects: slashes, explosions, auras, magic, weather, confetti and screen looks
- 51 stickers, 25 text looks and 18 text animations

ANIMATE YOUR WAY
- Simple frame-by-frame timeline for beginners, Advanced timeline with layers, folders, keyframes and easing
- Onion skins, motion paths and camera follow
- Pen and Shape tools: every drawing can be posed and animated
- 3D Shot: a 3D city with a physics-driven hero, rendered to video or turned into editable layers

AI THAT ANIMATES WITH YOU
- Connect Claude, Gemini or any app that supports MCP: it can add figures, apply moves and effects,
  move the camera and check its own work. Every AI change is a single undo step.
- Transcribe a voiceover on your PC and time the animation to every word

EXPORT
- MP4, WebM (with transparency), GIF, APNG, PNG/JPEG/SVG frames
- Opens .piv, .stk, .stknds and .nodes files

Your projects stay on your computer. No account needed.
